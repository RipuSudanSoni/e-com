About Ripu Sudan Jewellers

Ripu Sudan Jewellers was born from a simple belief — that every woman deserves to feel elegant, without compromising her budget.

We craft premium artificial jewellery using gold-plated alloys, anti-tarnish coatings, and skin-friendly materials. Each piece is designed in-house and handcrafted by skilled Indian artisans.

From daily wear to bridal sets, our collections are made to be worn, loved, and remembered.

Our Mission

Make premium-look jewellery accessible to every Indian woman.
